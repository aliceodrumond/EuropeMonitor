S&P Global Italy Composite PMI
Private sector output slowest in three months
Down from 53.6 in August, the S&P Global Italy Composite PMI
Output Index dropped to 51.0 in September. The latest reading
was consistent with modest growth in private sector output
that was the slowest in the third quarter.
For both activity and new business, growth was centred
on the service sector, as their manufacturing counterparts
signalled sustained, but softer contractions.
Growth in private sector employment slowed to a marginal
rate, while the latest reduction in backlogs was only marginal.
Rates of both input cost and output charge inflation rose to
four-month highs.
