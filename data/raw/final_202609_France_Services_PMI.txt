S&P Global France Composite PMI
The S&P Global France Composite PMI Output Index* rose
to a 25-month high of 51.1 in September, from 48.5 in August,
taking it above the 50.0 no-change level to signal renewed
growth in private sector business activity.
Higher output was attained in the absence of sales and
employment growth. New business and workforce numbers
both decreased marginally in September.
Expectations meanwhile softened, reflecting weaker
sentiment in the service sector.
As for prices, latest survey data signalled a steep
acceleration of inflation.
