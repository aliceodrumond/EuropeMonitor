S&P Global Germany Composite PMI
The S&P Global Germany Composite* PMI Output Index
registered 53.8 in September, up from 51.8 in August and its
highest reading for almost a year.
Output growth was broad-based and led by manufacturing,
as was the case for new business and export sales. Goods
producers also drove an increase in business expectations.
Employment rose for the second month running, albeit only
fractionally, as a further uptick in services workforce numbers
coincided with a slowdown in factory job cuts.
Rates of input and output price inflation both ticked up to a
four-month high, meanwhile.
